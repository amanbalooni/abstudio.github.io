01 / Overview

Food labels are full of information. Understanding them shouldn't be.

IngredLens translates ingredients, nutrition and health signals into a clear, personal experience. It gives people the confidence to make choices that work for their bodies and their lives.

02 / The problem

Everyday decisions hide behind unfamiliar words.

People want to eat better, but product labels ask them to decode tiny type, technical ingredients and nutrition tables in the middle of a busy shopping moment.

Ingredients are difficult to scan and compare.
Allergens and hidden sugar are easy to miss.
Generic information does not reflect personal needs.
03 / Research & discovery

Start with the questions people ask in the aisle.

Research focused on the gap between wanting to make an informed choice and having the time or context to do it.

01What is actually in this product?
02Is it safe for my dietary needs?
03Is there a better alternative?
04Can I understand it quickly?
Key insightPeople do not need more data. They need the right meaning at the right moment.

15 / Outcome

Less decoding. More choosing.

Simplified product discovery and scanning.
Faster understanding of ingredients and nutrition.
Personalized warnings that respect user context.
A clearer path toward more confident food decisions.
16 / Takeaways

What I learned.

01

Complex information becomes approachable when the hierarchy follows the user's questions.

02

Warnings can be useful and calm when they explain the why and offer a next step.

03

Personalization is a way to reduce cognitive load, not just a feature.

04

Good product design makes room for confidence, not just efficiency.
