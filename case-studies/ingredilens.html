01 / Overview

IngredLens is a personalized food and beverage scanning application designed to help people make safer and healthier purchasing decisions.

Users can scan packaged food products to instantly understand their ingredients, nutritional values, allergens, and potential health risks. By considering a user's medical conditions, allergies, and dietary preferences, IngredLens transforms complex food labels into simple, personalized guidance:

Safe. Caution. Avoid.

The goal was to design an experience that answers one simple but important question:

“Is this product safe for me?”
