IngrediLens

A clearer way to understand what is inside the food and products we bring home.

RoleProduct designerFocusResearch, UX, UITypeConsumer mobile experience
Know what you eat.

The brief

Make labels feel less like homework.

IngrediLens turns dense ingredient lists into useful, human-readable guidance. The experience helps people scan a product, understand its ingredients and make a confident choice without sorting through unfamiliar jargon.

I shaped the information hierarchy around the moments that matter: scan, understand and compare. The result is a calm utility that gives context first and detail when it is needed.
